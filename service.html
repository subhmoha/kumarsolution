ALUMINIUM FORMWORK REFURBISHMENT PROCESS

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Kumar Solutions follows a systematic refurbishment process to restore old aluminium formwork panels into reusable construction systems.

01. Drawing

Preparation of project drawings, old and new module drawings, material planning and area statements.

02. Inspection

Sorting reusable panels, identifying damaged materials, inward verification and quality inspection.

03. Chipping

Removal of hardened concrete deposits and unwanted material from aluminium panels.

04. Dipping

Panels are dipped into chemical tanks to remove remaining concrete residue and restore surface quality.

05. Chemical Cleaning

Deep cleaning process to remove stains, deposits and contaminants from aluminium formwork.

07. Leveling

Straitening of panels, Remove undulations (if any) By using Hydraulic press machine

06. Welding

Welding is done for cut or Damaged part. Equipment used: Welding Machine.

08. Buffing

Making surface to receive the lacker coating. With the help of Buffing Machine.

09. Coating

To achieve smooth finish and easy removal of panel Coating with lacker is done Equipment used: 1. Air Compressor 2. Air Pipe 3. Paint Gun

09. Mock-Up

Mock-up of flats and staircase, lift. Check if any Modification required for hole matching.
