Understanding Aluform Technology

Aluminium formwork technology is an advanced construction system used for rapid and repetitive casting of concrete structures. It enables faster construction cycles, improved dimensional accuracy and superior finishing quality.

The lightweight aluminium panels are durable, reusable and designed to increase productivity while reducing overall project costs.

Re-use Standard Operating Procedure

Kumar Solutions follows a structured Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for aluminium formwork reuse. Every panel is inspected, cleaned, repaired and maintained before being deployed to the next project.

This ensures maximum life, consistent quality and optimal performance throughout multiple reuse cycles.
