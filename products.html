Aluminium Formwork Products & Accessories

Aluminium Formwork Systems & Accessories

Kumar Solutions is an aluminium formwork manufacturer, producing formwork panels, components and accessories for residential, commercial and infrastructure construction projects. Established in 2006, we manufacture the products shown in this catalogue and support customers with product selection and project requirements.

Our aluminium formwork range includes wall panels, slab panels, beam components, corner angles, prop heads, ties and other system components. We also manufacture the accessories required for assembly, alignment, connection and handling of aluminium formwork systems.

Planning a new formwork requirement or need components for an existing system? Share your drawings, specifications and quantities with our team so we can discuss your requirements and prepare a suitable quotation. Product dimensions and configurations should be confirmed against your project requirements.
